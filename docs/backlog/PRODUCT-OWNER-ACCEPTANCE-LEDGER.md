# Product Owner acceptance and comments ledger

**Snapshot:** 2026-10-13. The candidate inventory was captured before creating this ledger PR (121 open PRs inspected). The EP01-S05 target was refreshed afterward to its current exact head. Listed PRs are candidate implementation/test slices, not claims that a story is complete.

**Purpose:** capture Product Owner decisions and comments against an exact story acceptance target. This ledger records only Product Owner signoff; it does not replace the authoritative story register.

## Authority and use

- Story IDs, titles, priorities, and acceptance criteria come from [the authoritative backlog](HACKATHON-MVP-EPICS.md). Dependencies and accountable skills come from the [routing graph](../operating-model/STORY-EXECUTION-ROUTING.json).
- Overall implementation and acceptance status remains in the [story execution register](STORY-EXECUTION.md). This ledger is the separate record of the Product Owner's pass/fail decision and comments.
- `Pending` is an initial placeholder meaning no Product Owner decision is recorded. It is not a pass, story acceptance, or a substitute for independent technical verification.
- Only the Product Owner should change `Pending` to `Pass` or `Fail`. Record the decision date, comments, and exact reviewed commit SHA. Link evidence that covers the original acceptance criteria.
- A PR can contain only one implementation or test slice. Its presence, merge, or approval does not by itself prove the complete story. Where no complete review target exists, leave the decision pending.
- A decision applies only to the exact reviewed revision. If the candidate revision or acceptance scope changes, preserve the prior entry and add a new dated entry for the new revision.
- Deployment approval is separate. A Product Owner pass does not authorize an Azure DEV change; exact change-specific repository-owner approval and release gates remain required.
