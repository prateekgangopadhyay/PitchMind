## Requirement and observed result

The welcome route now asks users to find a match and inspect its evidence. Its action is “Check match availability” and leads to `/home`, where the canonical fixture list shows its actual loading or unavailable state. This removes a misleading implication that the welcome route has a simulated fixture ready to open. The link remains a native anchor.
