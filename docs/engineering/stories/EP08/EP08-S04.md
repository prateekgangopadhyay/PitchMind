### Detailed verifier contract and implementation boundary

`CandidateExplanationLineageChecker.Check(candidate, context)` is a synchronous deterministic Application-layer guard. It binds candidate metadata to the SHA-256 of the exact web-JSON packet bytes, as well as match, canonical/projection revisions, selected time, sequence watermark, source version/provenance/hashes and context-policy version. It recalculates the packet's actual serialized size, bounds candidate text and claim count, requires at least one known evidence ID for each claim, validates referenced evidence against packet time/revision/sequence limits, restricts periods to 1–5 and selected time to 0–5400 seconds, rejects duplicate evidence IDs, and requires the top-level narrative to equal the joined claim text. Evidence payloads must be bounded object/array JSON. Evidence payload text is parsed as data and never treated as instructions.

The result is `CandidateExplanationLineageCheck`, not `ValidatedExplanation`, and `CanBePublished` is always false. The check does **not** prove that cited events semantically entail candidate text or that score/player/team/causality/intent assertions are true. The negative test intentionally shows that a fabricated score can pass lineage and still remains non-publishable. A semantic support policy, football false-claim suite, host composition and independent red-team proof remain necessary before EP08-S04 acceptance. Do not wire this slice to API/UI or persistence.
