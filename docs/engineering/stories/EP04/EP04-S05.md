## Local golden test slice

The local regression golden pins the embedded `synthetic-v2` fixture generated with seed `123` and the final aggregate state produced by replaying its events in source order. The generated fixture SHA-256 is `4d0e5cba88bfaaf596946bf7ebb50dcca18262e0bed3506ededeaf440189d67b`; the serialized final `MatchState` SHA-256 is `e18412b8a6c72dba5a73f5dcebc01cacee644db0938750486cdb97a05736176b`. A corrupted period field fails with a diagnostic identifying the clock/period mismatch. Tests cover repeat identities, sequence order, goal idempotency, score, cards, substitutions, halftime orientation, malformed definitions, invalid roster transitions and incomplete tracking in the tactical detector.

The pure Domain `FixtureCorrectionHistory` overlay now resolves ordered, provenance-carrying correction records for synthetic fixture replay. Each replacement must preserve the original event ID, match ID, sequence, period, elapsed clock and occurred-at time; a later correction revision wins, while conflicting replacements at one revision fail. Replacing a synthetic home goal with a shot produces a deterministic corrected-state SHA-256 of `92e3808bd73bb4e6596128a78078fbeabf01b1f33ec807d1bafa32fc3c31d6ba`. This is a fixture-level regression contract only: it does not admit or persist canonical PostgreSQL corrections, capture the omitted `SimulationEvent` context in the ledger, or establish the candidate feed's source semantics.

All hashes are repository regression values, not Product Owner-approved football snapshots or evidence about the candidate Manchester United–Liverpool feed.
